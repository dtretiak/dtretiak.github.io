About Me

I'm a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Washington, working under the guidance of Dr. Steve Brunton and Dr. J. Nathan Kutz. My research focuses on the intersection of fluid dynamics, machine learning, and computational physics.

With experience at institutions like SpaceX, Raytheon Technologies Research Center, and Los Alamos National Laboratory, I've developed expertise in Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), physics-informed machine learning, and the application of AI to solve complex engineering problems.

I'm passionate about developing novel computational methods that can advance our understanding of complex physical systems while making simulations more efficient and accurate.
